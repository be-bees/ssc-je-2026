Thermodynamics ≈14 Q

  • Q = ΔU + W; work by gas is +. Isothermal: ΔU = 0, Q = W. Throttling: h const. Free expansion: Q = W = ΔU = 0.
  • Zeroth law → temperature. Kelvin-Planck: no engine turns all heat into work (PMM-2). Clausius: heat won't go cold → hot by itself.
  • ηCarnot = 1 − T2/T1 · COPR = T2/(T1−T2) · COPHP = COPR + 1. ⚠ Kelvin only. 527/27 °C → 62.5%.
  • n = 0 isobaric · 1 isothermal · γ adiabatic · ∞ isochoric. Polytropic W = (p1V1−p2V2)/(n−1); isothermal pV ln(V2/V1).
  • Air: R = 0.287 kJ/kg·K, γ = 1.4; cp − cv = R. Open system h = u + pv (pv = flow work).
  • Steam: triple pt 0.01 °C; critical 374.15 °C, 221.2 bar; h = hf + x·hfg; hfg @100 °C = 2257 kJ/kg. Mollier = h–s. Throttling calorimeter → x.
  • Rankine η ↑ with higher boiler p, superheat, lower condenser p, reheat, regeneration. ηBrayton = 1 − 1/rp(γ−1)/γ.
  • ∮δQ/T ≤ 0 (= 0 reversible). Reversible adiabatic = isentropic. Real processes: entropy ↑.
  • Steady flow: V2 = √(2Δh) (Δh in J/kg) = 44.72√Δh (kJ/kg).

IC Engines ≈13 Q

  • SI: Otto, spark plug, r 6–10, quantity governing (throttle). CI: Diesel, injector, r 14–22, quality governing. Hit-and-miss: whole cycles skipped.
  • 4-stroke: 1 power stroke / 2 rev (n = N/2). 2-stroke: every rev, needs scavenging, mist (petroil) lubrication.
  • Octane (iso-octane vs n-heptane) = petrol anti-knock. Cetane (cetane vs α-methylnaphthalene) = diesel ignition quality.
  • SI knock at end of combustion; CI knock at start (ignition delay). What cures one worsens the other.
  • Firing order 1-3-4-2. Morse test → IP of multi-cylinder. Willan's line → FP of CI. Rope brake BP = π(D+d)N(W−S)/60.
  • ηOtto = 1 − 1/rγ−1 (r 8 → 56.5%). r = (Vs+Vc)/Vc. Same r: Otto > Dual > Diesel. Same pmax: Diesel > Dual > Otto.
  • IP = pmLAnk/60, BP = 2πNT/60, ηmech = BP/IP, FP = IP − BP. Stirling: 2 isothermal + 2 isochoric (heat rejected at const T).
  • Battery ignition: spark voltage falls as speed rises. Magneto: rises with speed.

Boilers · Turbines · Nozzles · Compressors ≈14.5 Q

  • Fire-tube: Cochran, Lancashire, Cornish, Locomotive. Water-tube: Babcock & Wilcox, Stirling. High-pressure: Lamont, Benson (drumless), Loeffler, Velox.
  • Mountings (safety): safety valve, water-level indicator, pressure gauge, fusible plug, stop valve, blow-off cock, feed check valve.
  • Accessories (efficiency): economiser (heats feed water), superheater (steam above saturation), air preheater, feed pump, injector, steam trap (drains condensate).
  • Critical pressure ratio: air 0.528 · superheated steam 0.546 · saturated steam 0.577.
  • De Laval = impulse (pressure drops in nozzles only). Parsons = reaction, 50% degree. Compounding cuts rotor speed.
  • Compressor: isothermal = least work. Perfect intercooling: p2 = √(p1p3). ηvol = 1 + C − C(p2/p1)1/n, falls as clearance or pressure ratio rises. Surging: centrifugal at low flow.

Refrigeration & AC ≈11 Q

  • 1 TR = 3.5 kW = 210 kJ/min. COP = refrigerating effect / work (5 TR on 5 kW → 3.5).
  • VCR: compressor (isentropic) → condenser → expansion valve (isenthalpic) → evaporator.
  • Halocarbons: R-number + 90 → C,H,F: R-12 CCl₂F₂ · R-22 CHClF₂ · R-134a C₂H₂F₄. Inorganic R-7xx = 700 + molar mass: NH₃ R-717, water R-718, CO₂ R-744, SO₂ R-764.
  • Bell-Coleman = air cycle (aircraft). Absorption: NH₃–water, LiBr–water (water is the refrigerant).
  • Hermetic: motor + compressor in one welded shell. Semi-hermetic: bolted cover.
  • Saturated air: DBT = WBT = DPT. Sensible heating/cooling: ω constant.

Fluid Mechanics ≈22 Q

  • τ = μ du/dy, ν = μ/ρ. 1 poise = 0.1 Pa·s, 1 stoke = 10⁻⁴ m²/s. Liquid μ falls with T; gas μ rises.
  • p = ρgh (5 m water = 49.05 kPa). Absolute = gauge + atm. Gauge = above atm; vacuum = below. 1 atm = 101.325 kPa = 10.33 m water = 760 mm Hg.
  • Piezometer: no gas or negative p. U-tube: yes. Inverted U: small differences. Bourdon: gauge pressure.
  • Centre of pressure below centroid: hcp = x̄ + IG/(A x̄); does not depend on liquid density. Floating body stable if GM > 0.
  • Δp: drop 4σ/d · bubble 8σ/d · jet 2σ/d. Capillary h = 4σcosθ/ρgd; mercury falls.
  • A1V1 = A2V2 (half d → 4 × V). Bernoulli: steady, incompressible, non-viscous, along a streamline. TEL is V²/2g above HGL.
  • Venturi Cd 0.95–0.98 · orifice 0.6–0.65 · pitot V = √(2gh). Hg–water manometer h = 12.6x.
  • Re < 2000 laminar, > 4000 turbulent. Laminar pipe: f = 64/Re, Vmax = 2Vavg, velocity parabolic, shear stress linear (0 at centre).
  • hf = fLV²/2gD. Losses: sudden expansion (V1−V2)²/2g · exit V²/2g · entry 0.5V²/2g. Max power when hf = H/3.
  • No-slip: any real (viscous) fluid has zero velocity relative to a fixed wall.
  • Rectangular notch Q ∝ H3/2; V-notch Q ∝ H5/2.

Hydraulic Machines ≈8 Q

  • Pelton: impulse, high head, low Ns, tangential; best bucket speed u = V/2. Francis: reaction, medium head, radial/mixed. Kaplan: reaction, low head, high discharge, axial, adjustable blades. Draft tube: reaction only.
  • Ns: turbine N√P/H5/4 · pump N√Q/H3/4.
  • Centrifugal pump: priming, backward-curved vanes, NPSHa > NPSHr (no cavitation). Series → heads add; parallel → discharges add. Q ∝ N, H ∝ N², P ∝ N³. Double volute balances radial thrust.
  • Reciprocating pump: air vessel smooths flow; negative slip = actual > theoretical.

Production ≈6 · 12–14 in 2024

  • Allowances: shrinkage, draft, machining, distortion, shake (only negative). Riser freezes last; Chvorinov t ∝ (V/A)².
  • Investment = lost wax (wax pattern + ceramic shell). Die / permanent mould = metal mould, non-ferrous. Centrifugal = pipes.
  • Defects: blow holes (gas) · cold shut, misrun (low fluidity) · hot tear (restrained contraction).
  • Flame: neutral O₂:C₂H₂ = 1:1, ≈3200 °C · oxidising → brass, bronze · carburising → high-C steel.
  • TIG: non-consumable W. MIG: consumable wire. Arc welding: electrode is consumed. Spot/seam = resistance. Thermit = rails.
  • Brazing > 450 °C, soldering below; filler drawn in by capillary action; base metal not melted.
  • V = πDN/1000 m/min (D 25 mm, V 15.7 → 200 rpm). Taylor VTⁿ = C: n HSS 0.1 · carbide 0.25 · ceramic 0.5.
  • Chips: continuous (ductile, high speed) · discontinuous (brittle) · BUE (ductile, low speed). Merchant φ = 45° + α/2 − β/2.
  • Grinding: Al₂O₃ → steel; SiC → cast iron, non-ferrous. Order: abrasive, grain, grade, structure, bond.
  • EDM: conductive + dielectric · ECM: Faraday · USM: hard brittle · EBM: vacuum · LBM: any. Down = climb milling. Shaper: quick return.
  • Punching: punch = hole size, clearance on die. Blanking: die = blank size, clearance on punch. F = L·t·τ. Hot work above recrystallisation.

Materials · SOM · EM · ToM ≈11 Q

  • Fe–C: eutectoid 0.8% C, 727 °C (pearlite); eutectic 4.3% C, 1147 °C (ledeburite). Steel ≤ 2% C; low-C < 0.3%.
  • Anneal (furnace cool) softens · normalise (air) refines · harden (quench) → martensite · temper cuts brittleness.
  • BCC (α-Fe, Cr, W, Mo) APF 0.68, 2 atoms · FCC (γ-Fe, Al, Cu, Ni) 0.74, 4 · HCP (Zn, Mg, Ti) 0.74, 6.
  • Brass Cu+Zn · bronze Cu+Sn · Invar Fe+36% Ni · SS 18-8 (Cr-Ni) · HSS 18-4-1 (W-Cr-V).
  • E = 2G(1+μ) = 3K(1−2μ), μ ≤ 0.5. Sudden load: 2P/A. Thermal σ = EαΔT (free bar: 0).
  • BM max: SSB centre WL/4 · SSB UDL wL²/8 · cantilever WL, wL²/2. δ: WL³/48EI · 5wL⁴/384EI · WL³/3EI · wL⁴/8EI.
  • T/J = τ/r = Gθ/L, J = πd⁴/32, torsional rigidity GJ, P = 2πNT/60. Euler Le: H-H L · F-F L/2 · F-free 2L · F-H L/√2. Hoop pd/2t = 2 × long.
  • Angle of repose = friction angle, tan φ = μ. Range max at 45°. Sphere CG at its centre.
  • Belt T1/T2 = eμθ; Tc = Tmax/3. Grashof s + l ≤ p + q. Whitworth = single slider-crank. Stiffer Hartnell spring → less sensitive. Isochronous = zero speed range.
  • Failure: ductile → von Mises (best) or Tresca; brittle → max principal stress (Rankine). Soderberg most conservative.
